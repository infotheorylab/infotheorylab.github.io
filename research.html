Research

Our research group works on information theory, signal processing, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. We aim to develop foundational theory that guides the design of trustworthy AI and machine learning algorithms.

Our Vision

We believe that information theory is key for the responsible design of AI and machine learning systems, and that a theory-guided approach can massively outperform heuristics.

Our Approach

For any performance metric (precision, fairness, privacy), we ask not "How can a system be made better?" but "What is the absolute best a system can be?"

Core Information Theory

We explore the fundamental limits of communication, computation, and inference to build a principled foundation for intelligent systems.

Our work establishes the mathematical foundations that determine what's theoretically possible in data processing, transmission, and analysis. We use information-theoretic tools to characterize fundamental performance limits and then design algorithms that achieve these limits.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

We translate deep theoretical insights into practical AI and machine learning algorithms that learn, adapt, and make reliable decisions.

Our theory-guided approach to AI and machine learning focuses on three critical challenges: fairness, privacy, and reliability. We develop algorithms that don't just work well in practice, but come with mathematical guarantees about their performance and behavior.

Real-World Applications

Our work impacts diverse fields, from reliable AI and privacy-preserving machine learning to next-generation wireless networks.

We engineer AI and machine learning algorithms that promote privacy, safety, and reliability for all. Our research extends from healthcare and hiring systems to telecommunications and beyond, ensuring that information theory reaches exciting new application frontiers.
